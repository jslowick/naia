Spiritual and secretive members of one half of the [[Ostean Trade Consortium]].  Recently they have been buying raw materials from [[Lanshire]] and many other Neutral Island-states in very large quantities. [[Eastern Wind Isles]] and [[Western Wind Isles]] span both sides of the [[2-Oshanan Trench|Trench]], but many of their cities are east, while Forts and Ships are west.  Winderspeakers generally don't allow outsides close.
