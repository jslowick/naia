#ship #neutral #order_lantern #grim_mercy

Ship owned by [[Order of the Sacred Lantern]].  Transports dangerous people, plague victims, etc.  Also the only [[Order of the Sacred Lantern|Lantern]] ship with cannons, though it has never fired them supposedly.

His crew are all Masked in Plague Doctor masks.  Shroud is the only one with a visible face.
