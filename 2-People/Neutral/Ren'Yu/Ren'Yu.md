#neutral #faction #renyu 
A race of blue skinned, or blotchy or blue tattooed skin, with pointy or fin like ears, and gills. They predominately live on the large island chain of [[Ereth'Yu]], though a very few live elsewhere, or have even joined pirate crews.  Seen as "primitive" by the OTC, [[Corites]], [[Merfolk]], and other [[3-Monsters|Sea Monsters]] seem to leave them alone, thus they are desired and captured as slaves, brought to [[Lanshire]] to harvest the coral there, which is sold to the [[Windspeakers]].

Slur: “U’s”
