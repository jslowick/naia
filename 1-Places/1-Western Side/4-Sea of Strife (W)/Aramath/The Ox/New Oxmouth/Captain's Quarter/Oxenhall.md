Found in the [[Captain's Quarter]] in [[New Oxmouth]] on [[The Ox]] in [[Aramath]].

The main thoroughfare of the quarter, lined with sturdy stone buildings and mansions of retired captains or wealthy merchants. Flags, banners, and carved figureheads mark entrances, giving it a nautical air. The street bustles with carriage traffic and servants, but retains an air of authority and prestige.  The new Commodore, [[Commodore Brant 'Grave-Eye' Malachai]] has already started redecorating.
