Notes: Different Islands with different factions

Important defensible island chain in the western [[4-Sea of Strife (W)|Western Sea of Strife]] that has major fortifications and guarding ships, you must cross near it to get deeper into the Pirate Iles.  The middle the water in [[Blackwater Depths]] of the 4 islands is similar to the [[2-Oshanan Trench|Trench]] in that you need a special coating on your ship to traverse is.
