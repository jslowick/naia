One of three harvesting flotillas, one in [[Atoll Ruina]], one in [[Atoll Volcanus]] and the last in [[Atoll Coralium]].  Each growing season, the three companies switch which Atoll and thus which Flotilla they will be using for the year.  The water in each Atoll, plus the previous years crop each add distinctive flavors to the Alcohol.
