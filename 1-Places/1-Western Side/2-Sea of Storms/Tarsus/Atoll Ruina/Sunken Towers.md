All over [[Atoll Ruina]] there are Sunken Ruins that are towers of some sort, some of them even jutting above the surface, which gives the hops, grapes, and sugarcane they grow in the waters a distinctive taste.  The towers make it difficult to use larger ships inside the Atoll.
