An ancient Temple on the northside of [[Atoll Ruina]] looking over [[Thunderfall Cove]] and [[Cloudcrash Cove]].  Its quite a tall building, and there are hundreds of steps to ascend it.  The temple, while older than anyone can remember does not look like ruins, pristine and untouched by the ravages of time.  Due to an accord with [[Crescent Bay]], it has been decreed by the Commodore of [[Ocearion]] that entry to the temple is forbidden without permission from the Commodore of [[Stormtide]].
