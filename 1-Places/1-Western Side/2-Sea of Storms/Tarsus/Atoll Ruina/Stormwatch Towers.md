These special lighthouses are not for warning ships away, but by some arcane sorcery or ancient technology, the Stormwatch Towers littered on [[Tarsus Major]] and [[Atoll Ruina]] illuminate when there is a storm coming.  This gives people warning and are able to prepare for them, which is one of the reasons [[Tarsus]] is able to sustain out so deep in the [[2-Sea of Storms|Sea of Storms]].
