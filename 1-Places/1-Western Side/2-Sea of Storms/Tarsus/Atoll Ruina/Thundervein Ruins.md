The ruins on the edge of [[Atoll Ruina]] here go deeper into the earth, and crackle with electricity.  It is said there is something at the bottom, but no one really knows.  They are generally avoided as not worth the hassle of trying to explore.  Sometimes those from [[Crescent Bay]] come and go into them and [[Tempest Keep]].
