The shoreline of [[Atoll Ruina]] here that faces the [[2-Sea of Storms|Sea of Storms]] has [[Merfolk|Mer-Sign]] and many sailors have reported seeing and hearing [[Sirens]] there.  The guess is there is a Merfolk settlement somewhere under the water, but usually they do not live in such close proximity with people without there being conflict, which is strangely absent.
