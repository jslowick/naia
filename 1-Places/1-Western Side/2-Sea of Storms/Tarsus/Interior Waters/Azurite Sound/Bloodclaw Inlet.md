Bay nearest to the [[Shifting Channel]] in [[Azurite Sound]] that harbors a very important species of small [[Grimshells|Grimshell]], called [[Bloodclaws]].  Their blood is harvested for the recipes for [[Tarsian Wine & Spirits]], and no one has found another Bay or location the crabs exist.  Strictly off-limits to everyone except authorized personnel.
