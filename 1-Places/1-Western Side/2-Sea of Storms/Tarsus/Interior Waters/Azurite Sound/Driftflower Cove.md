Cove where Oxeye Lilies are grown, and then used in [[Atoll Volcanus]] and the westernmost parts of [[Tarsus Major]].  The lilies absorb smoke and haze in the air, making it easier to breath.  This used to be a major trade between [[Aramath]] and [[Tarsus]], but sfter they cut [[Pirate Kingdom|Pirates]] direct trade, so did the lillies, so they make due.  Rumor is [[Merrick the Grey]] is trying to restart the trade, keeping the pirates out of it.
