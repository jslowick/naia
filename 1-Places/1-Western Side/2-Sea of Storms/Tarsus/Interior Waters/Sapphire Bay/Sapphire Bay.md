Large bay that encompasses the eastern side of [[Tarsus Major]] to the northern tip of [[Atoll Ruina]].  The Bay also contains the [[St. Erasmus' Asylum]] Island, which was gifted to the [[Order of the Sacred Lantern]].  One of the few places on Naia where gemstones can be found, besides [[The Crystal Isles]].
