While normally the bay would get shallower as it got closer to the landmass of [[Tarsus Major]], the corner of the bay that is right before land has a huge drop-off.  If ships could get to it easier, it would be a good place for [[Deepwater Docks]].  The area is so deep, no one has been able to find the bottom.
