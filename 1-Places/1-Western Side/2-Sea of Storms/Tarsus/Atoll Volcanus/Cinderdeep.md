All over [[Atoll Volcanus]] there are Active/Inactive Volcanoes, Steam Vents and other Geothermal events.  These give the hops, grapes, and sugarcane they grow in the waters a distinctive taste.  The Cinderdeep makes it difficult to use larger ships inside the Atoll, but out of the three, it is the safest, if there were an easier way to get inside it.
