Member: [[Pirate Kingdom]]
Notes: No large Human settlements

On the outer edge of the [[1-Sea of Mists|Sea of Mists]] in between the many of the main pirate isles and their Capital is a small band of shoals, that has wrecked many a warship, and even seasoned travelers.  It is said you must have a magical compass to get through it to [[Ashander's Folly]].
