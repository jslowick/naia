High above the [[The Dread Towers]], is a "temporary" Rope Bridge that connects them both.  It has fallen into somewhat disrepair, as it was always meant to be replaced with something more permanent.  Now it is mainly used as a dueling spot, or where illicit deals and meetings take place.
