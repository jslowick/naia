Named after the two pirate lords, [[Antonius Dread|Antonius]] and [[Castor Dread]], that built them.  The worry always was that they needed a second exit to [[Tortuga Harbor]], but that also could be a liability in an attack.  When [[Deephaven]] was discovered and settled, they took a bunch of the leftover rocks from mining metal ore and went about building two large towers, then constructed a great portcullis, [[The Dreadgate]], to go between them.
