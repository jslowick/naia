The walls of the [[The Brethren Court]] are said to be impenetrable.  During [[The Great War]] multiple ships unloaded full cannon fire  on it and they survived the assault.  It is unclear who exactly built them and how, though claims that the [[Ancient Mariner (Missing)|Ancient Mariner]] reproduced them in [[Tarsus]] before his disappearance.
