Member: [[Neutral]]
Notes:

Last Island before the [[2-Oshanan Trench|Trench]] on the Southeast side of the [[1-Sea of Strife (E)|Eastern Sea of Strife]], Glowing.  Past that to the south is the [[4-Sea of Night|Sea of Night]], where the sun doesn’t come out at all, so it acts like a magical lighthouse.  Pirates often wait in lure here for those [[Ostean Trade Consortium|OTC]] foolhardy enough to try to make the run from [[Ereth'Yu]] directly to [[Lanshire]].
