Notes: No large Human settlements

On the edge of the [[4-Sea of Night|Sea of Night]] near [[Lanshire]], there is a humongous Tree on a single Island.  For years, [[Duke Corwin Varchek]] would hang pirates there as a warning, but even the Lanshires won’t go near the tree anymore.  The tree has a Malevolent aura to it, and is said to be [[Haunted]].  It used to be fully in the [[1-Sea of Strife (E)|Eastern Sea of Strife]], but the Sea of Night has expanded to cover it.
