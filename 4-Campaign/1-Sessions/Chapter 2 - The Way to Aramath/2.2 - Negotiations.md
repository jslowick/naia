The players have huddled and have a plan for negotiations

[[Veronica 'Bellringer']] brings up [[Zephyrix Penelope 'Penny' of the North Wind]] and [[Jacques Courtemer]] too early.  They are scared and want to renegotiate.  Penny calls [[Primrose]] a monster.

Locke agrees to negotiate again, they are to be left with their [[Laminated Dingy]] and nothing else right now, a letter of marque to be owed based on current hostage rates.  That means the crew of [[The Wayward Bounty]] gets to keep the really nice [[Masterworked Laminated Cutlass]] and [[Double Barreled Blunderbuss]].  Locke agrees.

This annoys [[Fid]] in that his time for the last hour has been wasted and he didn't have a chance to counter offer.  [[Primrose]] even offers to owe a favor, which seems big for her, but Locke is adamant and shook on it.

As they are leaving, they see [[The Red Darling]] approaching, and veers towards [[The Animus]].  The crew decides to go back and save the Animus.  

Naval fight ensues, and [[The Wayward Bounty]] drives the [[The Red Darling]] to flee.  Instead of immediately pursuing it, they stop to help the Animus which is sinking from cannon fire.

On the Animus, below decks, they find [[A Strange Box]].
