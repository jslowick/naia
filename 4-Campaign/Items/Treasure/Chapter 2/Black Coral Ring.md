Requires Attunement. This item is [[Cursed]] to you after you attune to it.  

Once Attuned, this item will begin to change you, and for the rest of the Chapter it must keep it wet with salt water once every hour.  Failure to do so will cause the item to crumble to dust.

This item will change you into giving you a creation only power.
Note you can choose OOG, but IG you cannot.
Once fully changed, you get a bonus based on the power.

Witchborne - Can see in the Dark or while Blinded
Influential Family - Gain 200 [[Pieces of Eight]]
Evil Eye - 1/session Gain Insight on a person
Mistwalker - Gain +1 Physical Stress
Ren'Yu - 1/chapter You can take your crew underwater for a short time.
Haunted - Gain a ghostly ally
Special - Varies
